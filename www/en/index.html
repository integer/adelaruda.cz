About me

Relationships are at the heart of our wellbeing and the source of our greatest struggles. In my practice I focus both on what happens between people, and on the relationship you have with yourself.

I will help you develop a kinder attitude towards yourself, so that in difficult moments you can offer yourself support rather than criticism. I will guide you towards honest and safe relationships with others that do not leave you depleted. I believe that good relationships strengthen your ability to navigate life with all its challenges.

Education and experience

My professional foundation is built on a degree in psychology from Charles University and a comprehensive psychotherapeutic training in Gestalt therapy. I continuously develop my knowledge in areas relevant to my practice, currently focusing on couples therapy and work with adolescents. Drawing on this background, I offer an approach that is professionally grounded yet human and tailored to your unique situation.

Services

My work is grounded in the principles of Gestalt therapy. Gestalt believes that change comes when we better understand ourselves and the situation we are in, and when we can see our own needs and resources. It does not aim to judge or interpret, but to understand. It does not offer universal advice, but helps you find your own solutions.

In practice, this means I will not be a silent listener. I will engage in an active dialogue with you, offer feedback and help you become aware of what is happening in you right now. The goal is not merely to talk about the past, but to find your inner resources for a better present.

The first session is about getting to know each other. You will describe what brings you here, and I will show you how we can work on your topic together. After three sessions we will assess together whether my approach feels right for you.

Thanks to online sessions and therapy in English, I can support people who travel frequently or live in an international environment.

Individual therapy

Individual therapy is a space where you do not have to face your struggles alone — whether you are dealing with a specific diagnosis such as depression or burnout, or simply feeling a general unease and loss of meaning. I will help you explore what you need in your life right now, and find the resources to make your life feel fulfilling.

What you might come with:

  • Relationship with yourself: I don't feel comfortable in my own skin.
  • Relationship with others: I struggle in relationships — with a partner, friends, parents or children. I feel lonely.
  • Challenging life situations: Life feels overwhelming right now and I need support.
  • Dissatisfaction: I am unhappy and don't know what to do about it.
  • Emotional burden: I have experienced trauma and want to work through it. I am struggling with anxiety, depression or burnout.

Couples therapy

In couples therapy I create a neutral space for partners who want to restore mutual trust or navigate a difficult crisis together. I will help you look at your own needs and the needs of your relationship as a whole. Together we will look for ways to communicate and meet those needs clearly, so that your partnership can once again be a safe and fulfilling place.

You might come as a couple when:

  • There are issues you cannot agree on and need help resolving.
  • You are in a relationship crisis. Communication feels hard. You argue a lot.
  • There has been infidelity or another breach of trust.
  • You are considering a break-up and want to clarify whether to continue — or want guidance to end things well.

Terms & fees

For your first appointment, please get in touch by phone, email or the contact form below. The first session is about getting to know each other and agreeing on the terms of our work together. Further appointments will be arranged in person.

Effective therapeutic work depends on regularity. The standard frequency is once a week, or once a fortnight. We will agree on the right frequency together at the start of the process. I do not work at intervals longer than once a fortnight as I find it insufficiently effective.

Sessions can be cancelled free of charge at least 24 hours in advance by phone or email. Cancellations made later will be charged in full. Thank you for respecting my time and availability.

The fee is the same for in-person and online sessions, and for sessions in Czech or English.

Confidentiality is a core principle of my work. I treat all information about you as strictly private. I adhere to the ethical code of the European Association for Psychotherapy and supervise my work regularly to ensure a safe and professional approach for my clients. My work is currently supervised by Ing. Marian Chrasta and Mgr. Jana Besperátová.

I comply with personal data protection regulations.

In accordance with Act No. 101/2000 Coll. on the Protection of Personal Data and Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (GDPR), I hereby inform my clients about how their personal data is handled and processed.

  • I collect client data to the following extent: first name (surname is not required), phone number, and optionally email address — solely for the purposes of mutual communication (rescheduling or cancelling sessions).
  • This data is processed only by me and is not shared with any third party.
  • During therapy I take personal notes in anonymised form (clients cannot be identified from these notes, and they are not linked to the personal data listed above). They are therefore not personal data under Act No. 101/2000 Coll. and are not subject to the personal data processing measures under the above-mentioned regulations. These notes are used solely for my therapeutic work and are never accessible to third parties.
  • I am not a clinical psychologist and therefore do not maintain medical records as required by the relevant legislation.

These terms and fees apply to all sessions and help us maintain clarity, mutual respect and the quality of our work together.
