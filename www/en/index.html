About me

Relationships are the foundation of our wellbeing and the source of our greatest struggles. In my practice, I focus both on what happens between people and on the relationship you have with yourself.

I will help you develop a kinder attitude towards yourself, so that in difficult moments you can offer yourself support rather than criticism. I will guide you towards honest and safe relationships with others that will not leave you feeling drained. I believe that good relationships will strengthen your ability to cope with life and all the challenges it brings.

Services

My work is grounded in the principles of Gestalt therapy. Gestalt therapy holds that change happens when we better understand ourselves and the situation we are in, and can see our own needs and resources. It does not aim to judge or interpret, but to understand. It does not offer universal advice, but helps you find your own solutions.

In practice, this means I won’t just be a silent listener. I will engage in an active dialogue with you, offer feedback and help you become aware of what is happening in you right now. The goal is not merely to talk about the past, but to find your inner resources for a better present.

Individual therapy

Individual therapy is a space where you don’t have to face your struggles alone, whether you are dealing with a specific diagnosis such as depression or burnout, or feeling a general sense of unease and a loss of meaning. I will help you explore what you need in your life right now, and find the resources to make your life feel fulfilling.

What I can help you with

  • Relationship with yourself: I don't feel comfortable in my own skin.
  • Relationship with others: I struggle with relationships. With my partner, friends, parents or children. I feel lonely.
  • Challenging life situations: Life feels overwhelming right now and I need support.
  • Dissatisfaction: I am unhappy and don't know what to do about it.
  • Emotional distress: I have experienced trauma and want to work through it. I am struggling with anxiety, depression or burnout.

Couples therapy

In couples therapy, I create a neutral environment for partners who wish to rebuild mutual trust or navigate a difficult crisis together. I will help you gain insight into your own needs and those of your relationship as a whole. Together, we will look for ways to communicate and meet those needs, so that your partnership can once again be a safe and fulfilling place.

As a couple, you may find yourselves thinking

  • There are issues we can’t agree on and we need help resolving them.
  • We are going through a crisis in our relationship. Communication feels hard. We argue a lot.
  • There has been an affair or another breach of trust.
  • We are considering a break-up. We want to clarify whether to continue. We want to break up and we want a guide to help us do it properly.

Terms

Regularity
Effective therapeutic work depends on regularity. The standard frequency is once a week, or once a fortnight. We will agree on the right frequency together at the start. I do not work at longer intervals as I find them insufficiently effective.
Cancellation
You may cancel a session free of charge at least 24 hours in advance, by phone or email. If you cancel later, I will charge the full amount. Thank you for respecting my time and availability.
Insurance contributions
I am involved in preventive health insurance programmes that offer contributions for psychotherapy, for example VZP provides up to CZK 5,000 for 10 sessions. I recommend checking the current terms directly with your insurance provider.
Confidentiality and supervision
Confidentiality is a core principle of my work. I treat all information about you as strictly private. I adhere to the ethical code of the European Association for Psychotherapy and have my work regularly supervised to ensure a safe and professional approach. My work is currently supervised by Ing. Marian Chrasta and Mgr. Jana Besperátová.
I adhere to data protection principles.

In accordance with Act No. 101/2000 Coll. on the Protection of Personal Data and Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (GDPR), I hereby inform my clients about how their personal data is handled and processed.

  • I collect client data to the following extent: first name (surname is not required), phone number, and optionally email address, solely for the purposes of mutual communication (rescheduling or cancelling sessions).
  • This data is processed only by me and is not shared with any third party.
  • During therapy I take personal notes in anonymised form (clients cannot be identified from these notes, and they are not linked to the personal data listed above). They are therefore not personal data under Act No. 101/2000 Coll. and are not subject to the personal data processing measures under the above-mentioned regulations. These notes are used solely for my therapeutic work and are never accessible to third parties.
  • I am not a clinical psychologist and therefore do not maintain medical records as required by the relevant legislation.
